Bathroom & toilet · tile colour

The bathroom walls outside the shower, over the Bremen Ivoor 60 × 120 floor: three columns of 60 that carry the floor joints up the wall, and a 20 cm strip at the ceiling (120 + 120 + 20). Bremen is lighter and whiter than the Petra sample, as Ceramic Connection's own photos show it. The 60 × 60 candidates from Tegels in Huis (Babilon bone: the colour of Petra; Belavu ivoor: the warmest; Babilon wit, Batela wit), on the walls and on the floor, are in the walk-through's Finishes panel.

The top of the ledge: a sill of the Bremen floor tile (the pick) or the Rio on top as well. Both follow the tile colour above.

The toilet uses the same tile as the shower, so the two always match. The glass panel now stands on the sink side, and the rain head, hand shower and thermostat are on the mirror wall. You walk into the shower from the radiator side. Across the back of the shower a solid ledge wall, 1.00 m high and 15 cm deep, replaces the niche: four full rows of tiles, so its joints run on from the wall behind it, straight into a square metal edge profile (its 10 mm side on top, so from the door you see only a thin line), and the drain sits right in front of it.

Wall tile
Ceramic Connection Rio, glazed 6 × 25 cm, stacked vertically with grout in the tile colour, in shower and toilet. Shortlist: Turquoise, Blue, Clay
Floor + walls
Floor: Bremen Ivoor 60 × 120 cm (R10, 10 mm): the dry floor 3 × 60 by two equal 91 cm cuts with one joint down the middle; the shower floor its own sloped field to the linear drain; the toilet floor one tile across (3 tiles). Walls: Bremen standing, 120 + 120 + a 20 cm strip at the ceiling, three tiles per wall. The 60 × 60 candidates (walls and floor, the shower curb in the same tile) are in the walk-through
Shower ledge
Solid wall over the full back wall, 1.00 m × 15 cm deep, a square metal edge profile along the front (thick side on top). Top: a sill of the floor tile, Bremen Ivoor, in two ~90 cm pieces with one joint in the middle, in line with the floor's centre joint (the pick). The walk-through also has the Rio wrapped over the top
South wall
Washer column in the corner by the door, towel radiator next to it
