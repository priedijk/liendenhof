Bathroom & toilet · tile colour

The toilet uses the same tile as the shower, so the two always match. The glass panel now stands on the sink side, and the rain head, hand shower and thermostat are on the mirror wall. You walk into the shower from the radiator side. Across the back of the shower a solid ledge wall, 1.02 m high and 15 cm deep, replaces the niche: four rows of tiles, so its joints run on from the wall behind it, and the drain sits right in front of it.

Wall tile
Ceramic Connection Rio, glazed 6 × 25 cm, stacked vertically with grout in the tile colour, in shower and toilet. Shortlist: Turquoise, Blue, Clay
Floor + walls
Bremen Ivoor 60 × 120 cm (R10): walls standing, 70 / 120 / 70 from floor to ceiling, three tiles per wall; the dry floor 3 × 60 by two equal 91 cm cuts with one joint down the middle; the shower floor its own sloped field to the linear drain; the toilet floor one tile across (3 tiles). The walk-through also has 120 + 120 + 20 and Petra Ivoor 80 × 80
Shower ledge
Solid wall over the full back wall, 1.02 m × 15 cm deep. Top: a sill of the floor tile, Bremen Ivoor, in two ~90 cm pieces with one joint in the middle, in line with the floor's centre joint (the pick). The walk-through also has the Rio wrapped over the top
South wall
Washer column in the corner by the door, towel radiator next to it

Changed in this round

  • A tiled ledge wall across the back of the shower instead of the niche: 1.02 m high, 15 cm deep, with the linear drain in front of it. On top a sill of the floor tile (Bremen Ivoor, two pieces, one joint in line with the floor's); the Rio wrapped over the top is the alternative (a switch in the walk-through).
  • Two Rotterdam line-art prints (50 × 70, black frames) over the sofa on the Warm sage wall: the Erasmusbrug on the left, the Euromast on the right, centred on one line 1.42 m up.
  • Bathroom tiles laid as planned: Bremen Ivoor 60 × 120 standing on the walls (70 / 120 / 70), the floor in two equal 91 cm cuts with one centre joint, the toilet floor in 3 tiles.
  • The white balcony option is now painted brick: the bond and joints stay visible under the paint.
  • The paint plan, light to dark: living room warm white with Warm sage behind the sofa and on the hall-door wall, study greige with a darker sage (≈ F&B Lichen) behind the desk, bedroom olive sage (≈ F&B Treron) on every wall. Hall, toilet, storage and ceilings white.
  • Shower and toilet in Ceramic Connection Rio, 6 × 25 cm, stacked vertically with grout in the tile colour. Three candidates to compare: Turquoise (muted green), Blue (grey teal) and Clay (terracotta).
  • Oak cat shelves: a climbing route to a walkway on the sage living-room wall, and two shelves in the study by the cat tree.

In the walk-through

  • Look at a door and press E to open it. Ring the doorbell from the landing.
  • Press P or use the paint panel, then click a wall to repaint it.
  • The Finishes panel switches paint, tiles (colour and horizontal/vertical stack), the shower ledge top and floor. Press L for evening, M to move furniture.
  • Click a cat to pet it.
